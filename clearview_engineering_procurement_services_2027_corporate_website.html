Company Overview & Mandate

Built on Technical Rigor, Reliable Execution & Local Expertise.

Clearview Engineering & Procurement Services (CEPS) is based in Freetown, Sierra Leone. We provide integrated engineering consultancy, construction and project management, strategic procurement, and enterprise technology services for small and medium-scale assignments.

Founded in 2020 and formally incorporated in Sierra Leone in 2022, CEPS executes defined technical assignments—ranging from structural integrity assessments, architectural building designs, and specialized equipment supply to on-site construction supervision and skilled trade coordination.
